What's actually different

Four things nothing else on your phone does.

It finishes the job.

Every finance app can tell you about the $54.99 charge. Almost none will cancel it, sit through the retention flow, and confirm it's done. The gap between knowing and doing is where the money sits, and that gap is the entire product.

Built for premium cards.

Nothing else tracks Platinum and Sapphire Reserve credits, activates merchant offers for you, and warns you before they expire. That's an ignored category worth several hundred dollars a year to the people who carry these cards.

Credit file, not just bank feeds.

Combining Plaid with Spinwheel means Amalgamic sees cards, balances and liabilities that transaction data alone misses. Most people are surprised by at least one thing on the list.

Keeps everything it recovers.

Several products take a percentage of what they get back, which rewards chasing large recoveries and ignoring small ones. Amalgamic charges a flat subscription. Everything recovered is yours.

Who it's for

This is built for you if:

  • You carry two or more annual-fee cards.

    Between them there are credits, offers and protections you've never fully used, because using them is a part-time job. This is where most of the value sits.

  • Your household has forgotten subscriptions.

    Charges spread across four cards, some in your name, some not. Amalgamic sees them all in one list, including the ones on the card you barely use.

  • You travel enough to accumulate small disputes.

    Delayed bags, cancelled flights, duplicate hotel holds. Individually not worth the call. Collectively significant.

And honestly, it isn't for you if:

...you already track every credit and file your own disputes, or you don't carry annual-fee cards, or what you actually want is long-term budgeting and retirement planning.

We'd rather you knew now than after a month of paying for it.
