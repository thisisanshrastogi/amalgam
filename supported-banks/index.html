§01

How coverage works

Amalgamic does not integrate with banks one at a time. We connect through two regulated data providers, and our coverage is whatever theirs is — which in practice means the overwhelming majority of US institutions, down to small credit unions.

Primary

Plaid

Connects more than 12,000 financial institutions across the US — national banks, regional banks, credit unions and brokerages.

Specialist

Spinwheel

Liability and debt accounts — loans, lines of credit and balances that transaction feeds alone tend to miss.

When you link an account you authenticate with your bank through the provider, not through us. Larger institutions use OAuth, meaning you log in on your bank's own page and approve access there.

§03

If yours isn't listed

Try connecting anyway. Coverage is far wider than this page shows and it changes constantly. If the connection fails, email support@amalgamic.io with the institution name and we will tell you whether it is coming.

§04

Disconnecting an account

Control

Revocable from either end, at any time.

Disconnect an institution from your Amalgamic dashboard, or revoke access directly with Plaid or Spinwheel without involving us. Either stops the data flow. What we already hold is governed by the retention rules in the privacy policy.
